Application Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

BPMS (Business Process Management System) is developed by NATKRAM SYSTEMS. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, uses, and protects information when you use the BPMS software and its connected services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Basic account information (name, email) when signing in with Google.
  • Operational data entered by users inside BPMS modules.
  • Device information such as OS version and application logs.

3. Google OAuth Information

When you sign in using Google, BPMS receives your email address and basic profile information. BPMS does not access your contacts, files, or any other Google data.

4. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users securely.
  • To enable BPMS features tied to your account.
  • To improve system performance and reliability.

5. Data Sharing

BPMS does not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Data may be shared only when required by law or to protect system integrity.

6. Data Security

We implement industry‑standard security measures to protect your information, including encryption, secure authentication, and restricted access controls.

7. Your Choices

You may request deletion of your account data or revoke Google access at any time through your Google Account settings.
