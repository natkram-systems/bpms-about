Application Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

BPMS (Business Process Management System), developed by NATKRAM SYSTEMS, is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to safeguard your information when you use BPMS and its connected services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and basic profile details obtained through Google OAuth.
  • Operational Data: Any business or workflow information entered by users within BPMS modules.
  • Device & System Logs: OS version, device type, error logs, and performance metrics used for diagnostics.
  • Usage Information: Module activity, timestamps, and workflow interactions to improve system reliability.

3. Google OAuth Information

When signing in with Google, BPMS receives your verified email address and basic profile information. BPMS does not access your Google Drive files, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data outside of authentication.

4. How We Use Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users securely and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To enable BPMS features tied to your account and organizational role.
  • To improve system performance, stability, and workflow efficiency.
  • To diagnose errors, monitor system health, and enhance user experience.
  • To ensure compliance with organizational policies and audit requirements.

5. Data Sharing

NATKRAM SYSTEMS does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data may only be shared under the following conditions:

  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • To protect the integrity, security, and functionality of BPMS.
  • With authorized personnel for technical support or maintenance.

6. Data Security

We implement industry‑standard security measures including encryption, secure authentication, access controls, and continuous monitoring. While we strive to protect your data, no system is completely immune to risks. Users are encouraged to maintain strong passwords and safeguard their accounts.

7. Data Retention

Operational data is retained as long as your organization uses BPMS or as required by business policies. Logs and diagnostic information may be retained for system improvement and security monitoring.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following options regarding your data:

  • Request deletion of your account or operational data (subject to organizational approval).
  • Revoke Google OAuth access through your Google Account settings.
  • Request clarification on how your data is used or stored.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

NATKRAM SYSTEMS may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements, legal requirements, or new features. Continued use of BPMS after updates signifies your acceptance of the revised policy.
